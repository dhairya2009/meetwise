# 🚀 MeetWise

**MeetWise** is a smart, AI-driven web platform designed to eliminate meeting fatigue. It automatically converts meeting audio recordings into structured, actionable summaries, concise key points, and clear action plans.

By transforming hours of raw conversation into organized, prioritized to-do lists—complete with assigned responsibilities—MeetWise ensures that teams stay aligned, accountable, and highly productive.

### ⚠️ Project Status: Under Heavy Development

> **Note:** MeetWise is an ambitious, large-scale project currently in its active discovery and development phase. Architectural frameworks, technology stacks, and exact implementation models are actively being researched, evaluated, and prototyped.

## ✨ Key Features (Planned)

- **🎙️ Smart Audio-to-Text Transcription:** Seamlessly upload or record meeting audio and convert it into high-fidelity text.
- **🧠 AI-Powered Insights:** Automatically extract general summaries, structural outlines, and critical context.
- **🎯 Action Item Extraction:** Smart parsing of discussions to isolate specific tasks, operational priorities, and hard deadlines.
- **👥 Accountability Mapping:** Intelligently assign extracted tasks to specific meeting participants based on verbal commitments or context.
- **📊 Collaborative Dashboard:** A modern UI to view, organize, track, and export your team's generated to-do lists and meeting histories.
